Here’s a little help getting started.

What’s the difference between a Tree, a Branch, and a Nest?+

A Tree is your whole community. A Branch is an optional way to organize related Nests. A Nest is a chat. You can join multiple Trees and switch between them in My Orchard.

How do people join my Tree?+

Create your Tree and share an invitation link. Public Nests are discoverable by people inside that Tree; they aren’t public internet chats. Private Nests require an admin invitation.

Who can create Branches and Nests?+

Members can propose new spaces. Tree admins choose whether Branches and Nests need approval, and can assign admins to help look after individual spaces.

How does Churp protect messages?+

Messages and attachments are encrypted. By default, Churp manages the keys so signing in can restore access. That means Churp can recover messages addressed to managed-key members.

Self-managed keys are optional in your profile and require a personal recovery key. A Nest shows a shield when every joined member uses self-managed keys; new messages are then end-to-end encrypted. Earlier history keeps its original protection. Names, membership, and timestamps remain visible to the service.

Will new members see older messages?+

No. Joining a Nest gives someone access to messages sent after they join. Leaving and rejoining starts a new history boundary.

How do I add Churp to my phone?+

iPhone: Open Churp in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: Open it in Chrome, open the three-dot menu, then choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

For notifications on iPhone, use the installed Home Screen app on iOS 16.4 or newer. Enable device notifications in your Churp profile.

Is there a paid plan?+

Paid plans and purchases are not available yet. You can create an account without entering payment details. Plan details will appear here before billing launches.
